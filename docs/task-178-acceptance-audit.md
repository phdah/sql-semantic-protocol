## Parser semantic bug fixes and boundary disposition (2026-11-09)

**Three independent red/green investigations were added without weakening historical TASK-177 snapshots.** First, sqlparser dialects exposed `GROUP BY ROLLUP/CUBE` as either dedicated grouping ASTs or function-shaped group expressions. The ANSI path had previously emitted an **unsound feasible zero-output proof** when the source relation was empty. `src/analysis/aggregation.rs` now canonicalizes function-form ROLLUP/CUBE before constructing typed grouping semantics, and `src/analysis/queries.rs` excludes function-form supergroups from the one-row-per-regular-group shortcut. The first-party DuckDB native regression establishes a grand-total row for empty inputs, separate real-NULL versus synthetic subtotal identity and the contrasting HAVING rejection. This changes 21 formerly drifting sample/dialect public document cells (S100/S101/S104).

Second, `CAST(ROW(...) AS STRUCT(...))` had exactly the same conservative unknown semantics, but `sqlparser` produced inconsistent `STRUCT` display spellings (parenthesized versus typed fields) used in the diagnostic and typed Unknown reasons. `src/analysis/functions.rs` and `src/analysis/expressions.rs` now emit the same **stable, explicit unsupported STRUCT CAST reason**, without promising nested evaluation or native vendor certification. This targets historical S179/duckdb, one additional drift.

Third, `JOIN LATERAL ... ON TRUE` under the MSSQL dialect parses the unquoted `TRUE` as an actual **column identifier**, whereas ANSI treats it as the boolean constant. Translating either into the other without schema/parser evidence would be a false equivalence. `tests/task_178_remaining_drift.rs` asserts the concrete distinct predicates and retains both correlated sides as **Residual** for positive and rejected/absence physical proof. The retained S152/mssql drift is a named blocking parser-law boundary, not a silent parser success or approved native vendor claim.

The active [reconciliation overlay](task-178-canonical-reconciliation.json) lists the 22 targeted semantic/diagnostic fixes and one remaining deliberate drift, independently of immutable [TASK-177 historical snapshot](task-177-equivalence-snapshot.json). The expected historical 3,069 same / 23 different full documents become 3,091 same / one different **only after green final-head verification**. Green on the new candidate must not be misinterpreted as 3,091 independently certified output-bag proofs.

The frozen TOP/FETCH/MINUS masks observed in CI [42710748522](https://github.com/phdah/sql-semantic-protocol/actions/runs/42710748522) show 13 MINUS/EXCEPT identical public documents, 13 TOP/LIMIT and 13 FETCH/LIMIT differing full public documents, and 13 WITH TIES/LIMIT deliberately non-equivalent controls.
